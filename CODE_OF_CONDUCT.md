# Community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, sexual identity or orientation, or technical background.

Astrology software handles unusually personal data. Respect for consent,
privacy, and different cultural relationships to astrology is part of respectful
participation here.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, never a person's worth or intelligence.
- Use synthetic chart data in issues, tests, screenshots, and discussions.
- Ask before sharing another person's birth data or private correspondence.
- Credit sources and contributors.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully, including scope and licensing
  boundaries.
- Make room for newcomers and people communicating in a second language.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexualized attention, stalking, or
  intimidation.
- Doxxing or publishing personal, birth, account, or security data without
  explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption, bad-faith demands, spam, or deliberately derailing
  technical discussion.
- Impersonation, plagiarism, or knowingly misleading claims about calculation
  accuracy, privacy, licensing, or project endorsement.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests,
discussions, release channels, community events, and private communication made
in an official project capacity.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact method in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue containing sensitive
details.

Maintainers may edit or remove content; issue a private correction or warning;
temporarily restrict participation; or permanently ban participation. Decisions
consider impact, context, repetition, and community safety. Maintainers will
limit disclosure and protect a reporter's privacy as far as practical.

Project maintainers who do not follow this policy may be removed from
moderation or decision-making duties by the other maintainers.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant and adapted for a
privacy-sensitive astrology project.
